Our Table Collections

Every table starts as a raw slab from our warehouse floor. From there, it's finished one of two ways.

Live Edge Natural Wood

Solid slabs sanded smooth while keeping every natural curve, knot, and grain line intact. Thick, heavy, and built to be the centerpiece of the room.

Epoxy Tables

Custom-poured, high-strength epoxy resin fused with live edge wood to replicate rivers, coastlines, cosmic styles or even patterns or like a unique engraving. They are then finished to a mirror-glass shine but we can also do a natural protective and non shiny coat.
